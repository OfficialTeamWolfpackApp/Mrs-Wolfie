THE STORY
MORE THAN BOXING

Strength. Determination. Purpose.

For Zoey “Mrs Wolfie” James, boxing became more than simply stepping into a ring. It became a source of strength, confidence and purpose.

After experiencing domestic violence, boxing became an important part of rebuilding her confidence and finding strength again. Rather than allowing those experiences to define her future, she channelled them into something positive.

Together with Chris “Mr Wolfie” James, she co-founded Team Wolfpack. The aim is bigger than individual fights. It is about creating an identity built around strength, loyalty, unity and supporting people both inside and outside the ring.

As a boxer for Team Wolfie, Mrs Wolfie continues to represent Team Wolfpack whenever she competes.
